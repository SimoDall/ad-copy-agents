# Just-In-Case Bundle — Product Brief

## Overview

The Just-In-Case Bundle is a digital product (5 fillable PDFs, delivered as a download) sold under the Leave It Sorted brand. It's a plain-English family organizer built for the ordinary, unplanned days — a hospital stay, a bad flu, a work trip that runs long, a night a parent can't be reached — not for death or estate planning. The flagship is The Just-In-Case Family Binder, a 44-page fill-in-the-blank reference covering everything a stand-in caregiver would need to know about the household. It's sold with 4 shorter standalone bonus documents that can also be used on their own (a babysitter quick sheet, a partner's private financial/logistics briefing, a large-print grandparent card, and a pet care card). Every field in all 5 PDFs is a real, transparent AcroForm text field — fillable on a phone, tablet or computer, or printed and filled by hand.

## Brand Context

Leave It Sorted (leaveitsorted.com) is Simone's Shopify store selling digital "handover" organizers. Its existing flagship, The Ultimate Family Handover Kit, targets an older audience planning for end-of-life and estate handover. The Just-In-Case Bundle is a new product line on the same store and the same visual brand, but aimed at a different buyer and a different fear: not "what happens when I die" but "what happens if I'm suddenly out of action for a day, a week, or a month." Same design system and production quality as the existing kit; different audience, different emotional register, different content (no wills/estate language — school runs, babysitters, allergies, bills, bedtime routines).

## Target Audience & Core Problem

Primary buyer: young parents (new parents through parents of school-age kids), typically the household's default "organizer." The anxiety that drives the purchase is specific and common — not fear of death, but the ordinary "what if something happens and I'm not reachable" worry: a surgery, a bad accident, a sudden hospital stay, a work trip gone long, a night the usual parent genuinely can't be reached. The underlying pain point is that the information a stand-in caregiver (a partner, grandparent, babysitter, co-parent, or trusted friend) would need is scattered across someone's head, phone, and a dozen apps — and nobody else can reconstruct it under stress. Secondary buyers/use cases: grandparents or regular babysitters who want the family to hand them one, and co-parents/blended families who want a shared reference. The product sells relief from that specific anxiety — "if I'm not here, the house still runs" — framed as preparedness and care, not catastrophe.

## Positioning & Differentiation

Against generic "emergency binder" printables (common on Etsy) and free checklist templates, the bundle's edge is depth and execution: 20 full sections and ~700 real, computer-fillable form fields in the flagship alone, not a static printable. Its strongest differentiator is the Childcare & Caregiver Playbook section — the bedtime routine, meltdown triggers, screen-time rules, comfort objects, and house rules that almost no competing product bothers to capture, because it goes beyond "allergies and phone numbers" into how the family actually runs a day. Second differentiator: it's a bundle, not one document — the main binder is comprehensive, but the 4 bonuses are each independently useful and shareable (a sitter doesn't need the whole binder, just the fridge-ready quick sheet; a grandparent doesn't want 44 pages, they want one large-print card). That modularity is a real usability advantage over single-document competitors. Pets get their own standalone card rather than being an afterthought.

## Brand Voice & Design System

Voice: warm, plain-English, reassuring — never clinical or legal-sounding. Copy leans on short direct sentences, second person, and framing that normalizes the need ("this isn't about the worst day") rather than dwelling on catastrophe. Light touches of personality ("— that's normal") keep it from reading like a government form.

Palette: ink-forest #1E3327 (headers, dark accents), aged-brass #A97D34 (dividers, tags), warm-parchment #F1ECE1 (cover backgrounds), dusty-clay #B5715A (badges), charcoal-ink #26221D (body text).

Typography: Fraunces (serif, italic display weight) for titles; Lato (sans) for body copy and field labels.

Format hook worth using in marketing copy: every field is a real fillable PDF form field — open it, type, save — not a printable you can only write on by hand.
